# Supply Chain Visibility Optimization – Milestone 3

## Overview

Milestone 3 focuses on evaluating supplier performance and transportation efficiency using interactive Power BI dashboards. The objective is to monitor supplier reliability, purchase order fulfillment, transportation costs, carrier performance, and route efficiency to support strategic supply chain decision-making.

---

# Supplier Scorecard Calculation Methodology

The Supplier Performance Dashboard evaluates suppliers using key operational and quality metrics.

## Methodology

The supplier scorecard was developed by analyzing supplier-related transactions and purchase order data. Performance was measured using the following KPIs:

- Total Purchase Orders
- Purchase Order Fulfillment Rate
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Supplier Quality Performance
- Total Purchase Value
- Average Delivery Time

Each supplier's performance was assessed by comparing these KPIs across all suppliers. Suppliers with consistently high fulfillment rates, timely deliveries, and better quality performance received higher overall scores.

---

# Supplier Ranking and Benchmarking Approach

Suppliers were ranked based on multiple performance indicators rather than a single metric.

## Ranking Criteria

- Higher Purchase Order Fulfillment Rate
- Higher On-Time Delivery Percentage
- Better Product Quality Performance
- Lower Average Delivery Time
- Higher Purchase Order Volume

Benchmarking was performed by comparing supplier KPIs against overall supplier averages, allowing quick identification of top-performing and underperforming suppliers.

The dashboard includes supplier rankings to support procurement and vendor management decisions.

---

# Transportation Cost Analysis Methodology

The Transportation Analytics Dashboard evaluates logistics expenses and shipment efficiency.

## Metrics Analyzed

- Total Transportation Cost
- Cost per Shipment
- Total Shipments
- Transportation Cost by Carrier
- Transportation Cost by Route
- Monthly Transportation Expenses

Transportation costs were aggregated and analyzed across different carriers, routes, and time periods to identify cost-intensive logistics operations.

Cost per Shipment was calculated by dividing total transportation cost by the total number of shipments.

---

# Route and Carrier Performance Evaluation

Transportation efficiency was analyzed by comparing logistics performance across different routes and carriers.

## Route Performance

Routes were evaluated using:

- Total Shipments
- Transportation Cost
- Average Cost per Shipment
- Delivery Volume

Routes with consistently higher transportation costs or lower shipment efficiency were identified as potential optimization opportunities.

## Carrier Performance

Carriers were compared using:

- Total Transportation Cost
- Shipment Volume
- Average Cost per Shipment
- Delivery Performance

This comparison helps identify cost-effective logistics partners and supports carrier selection decisions.

# Key Insights

- A small number of suppliers consistently achieve the highest fulfillment and quality performance.
- Some suppliers experience lower on-time delivery rates, indicating opportunities for process improvement.
- Purchase order fulfillment varies across suppliers.
- Transportation costs differ significantly across carriers and shipping routes.
- Certain routes contribute disproportionately to logistics expenses.
- Cost per shipment varies depending on carrier selection and shipment volume.
- Monthly transportation expenses reveal seasonal logistics trends.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Strengthen partnerships with top-performing suppliers.
- Monitor low-performing suppliers and implement supplier improvement plans.
- Prioritize suppliers with higher fulfillment rates and consistent product quality.
- Optimize procurement strategies using supplier performance benchmarks.
- Negotiate transportation contracts with cost-efficient carriers.
- Review high-cost transportation routes and identify alternative logistics options.
- Continuously monitor transportation KPIs to reduce logistics expenses.
- Use dashboard insights for strategic supplier selection and transportation planning.
